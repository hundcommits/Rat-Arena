How to win: one long lane through the Moon Meadow, cut by a big river in the middle (it never slows you). Each team has three towers (cone, sniper, cannon) and a base. Every 26 seconds a line of minions marches out: two snakes, a big skeleton and two eagle riders (a fast mage skeleton jumps off each rider when it falls). Kill them for XP. Lose both towers and the enemy sends double lines (more XP for you to come back). The King Cobra in his fort gives +25% damage, the Giant Crab on the beach gives a shield as big as your health, both to whoever lands the last hit. At 6:00 the Cobra becomes the Skeleton Lord: beat him and he leads your next wave. Each side also has a small monster camp. Break the enemy base to win: there is no time limit.
Keys: move with WASD or the arrows, aim with the mouse (basic attacks go where you point), attack with R / F / left click, skills with Space and Shift, switch hunters with 1 2 3 in 2v2, pick level-up upgrades with Z X C.
Host: your browser runs the monsters and the rules, so keep the game tab open and in front for the whole match (a hidden tab pauses the island for everyone).
